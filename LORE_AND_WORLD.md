# RYDER'S ROAD - Premise

Ryder is the main playable character. He is a coder and game engineer
creatively blocked because his game contains many bugs and unfinished systems.
The player exists inside his code and repairs each broken section by completing
first-person block parkour and reaching the final Patch Block.

The player-facing title is `RYDER'S ROAD`. `RYDERS BLOCK` may remain as a
legacy internal codename in stable technical identifiers.

For current alpha phases this identity appears only in documentation,
branding, and temporary development labels:

- Void may be shown as `Null Space`.
- Checkpoint may be shown as `Restore Point`.
- Respawn may be shown as `Restore`.
- The laboratory finish may be shown as a temporary `Patch Block`.

Movement, camera, checkpoint, restore, ranking, save, and completion behavior
must remain independent from lore terminology. The project still contains no
narrative presentation system, cutscenes, message framework, ghosts, Crash
Wave, or lore-coupled gameplay rules.
