# ADR 0012 — Campaign production and presentation authority

Accepted 2026-09-06 from the Astra master production brief.

Normal Campaign successors come from the explicit stable-ID Campaign catalog,
never resource sorting. Spiral stays separate. A valid Bronze completion is
sufficient progression; scores/rewards remain stored but are secondary to
rank/time/PB in results. Additional campaign IDs are design-only until each
module is complete; see `Docs/ASTRA_PRODUCTION.md`.

Preserve the current motor and manual vertical look. Arms remain hidden.
Android supports both landscape directions with portrait forbidden. Missing
production audio is silent, with asset-driven cues and removable presentation
subscribers, not synthesized placeholder tones. Module 003 must establish the
quality standard before content production spreads to 001/002 or new modules.
Automated tests supplement rather than replace physical S23 acceptance.
