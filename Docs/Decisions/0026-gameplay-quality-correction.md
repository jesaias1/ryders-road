# ADR 0026 — gameplay quality after S23 rejection

2026-09-09. The user played the 0.12.0 slice on S23 and rejected its gameplay
standard: little variation, weak momentum incentives, first-try Diamond and
repetitive/unclear art. This supersedes earlier near-acceptance and delivery stops.
Continue production with the accepted controls; no Worlds 006–008, PC work,
movement rewrite, trial promotion or new unrelated systems.

Use the archived concepts for quality and the four S23 captures for failure
evidence. Keep the original pad art where the defect is runtime clutter; correct
the broad-support tiling path. Replace the flattened Restore shrine with a flat
corner signal and short activation pulse. The original shrine asset remains
available; the signal has no collision or checkpoint authority. New gallery skins must
be full support skins, never caps layered over another gameplay model.

Windward's uniform arc becomes differently shaped north galleries with a timed
pressure pair and a three-landing direct line. Foundry's opening gets separated
run-up/landing beats, broader optional transfer brackets and different crown beam
shapes. Its later ferry/cooling sequence keeps its established spacing; route
changes do not uniformly lengthen every gap. Preserve the five-road journey and
stable surviving block/Restore/Patch/module IDs. Retired Windward arc.09–11 and
skill.chord.4–5 remain recoverable in baseline Git; do not reuse their identities.

Separate new run PBs by stable module ID, content version, movement compatibility
and threshold version. V4 receives additive historicalRecords/versionedBests
arrays. Before writing a new completion, snapshot the legacy aggregate once;
keep existing aggregate progression/reward meaning and all version buckets.
Legacy version metadata was latest-run metadata, so never guess that it identifies
the legacy PB. Missing arrays normalize empty; no field is reinterpreted, schema
increment or destructive migration is needed. Test old JSON, reload, slower/faster
new routes, invalid runs and retained unlocks.

Times generated with deterministic real-motor input establish feasibility and
relative opportunity only. Use explicitly provisional candidate thresholds for
changed routes, document the input/run evidence and request physical calibration
through the delivered APK. Do not label fun, Diamond difficulty, art or sustained
60 FPS physically approved. STOP after the coherent APK/Git delivery for feedback.
