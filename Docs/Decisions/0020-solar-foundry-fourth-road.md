# ADR 0020 — Solar Foundry, fourth Campaign road

2026-09-08. Continue independent production under ADR 0019 and the user's explicit
instruction to proceed without the phone. One complete new world is the scope of
0.9.8; movement promotion remains a separate physical decision.

Append `module.004.solar-foundry` after `module.003.flow-error`. The pre-existing
`module.004.the-spiral` keeps its identity and remains outside Campaign. Never use
the displayed ordinal as identity. Save schema remains V4: new content adds a new
record only when played; existing records and PB metadata retain their meaning.
Any valid Bronze completion of 003 unlocks Foundry. Foundry is currently the final
Campaign road; completing it offers replay, never an accidental jump into Spiral.

The route rises through intake pipes, a transfer crane, reactor cooling platforms
and a radiator crown. One ferry has a meaningful gap-crossing purpose; three
optional brackets provide a faster, narrower bypass. Two crumble pads demand
forward commitment. Three Restore Points bound recovery cost. The Patch Block
ends the road at a service house. Attempt-local shards mark the optional line;
they do not gate completion or add persisted economy data.

All geometry is original baked low-poly construction. Near architecture has
matching mesh collision; distant collectors are explicitly scenery. Standard
landings retain the shared gameplay surfaces and color language. Architecture
sits below or beside the route; no invisible floors or helper rails are added.
Authoring values live in serialized assets; the Editor builder is an explicit
tool and is never invoked by routine startup. Existing authored worlds remain
unchanged. Append the new biome enum value to preserve serialized enum meanings.

Automated motor traversal, clearance, carry/recovery, progression and rendered
views qualify this as a playable development candidate. They do not establish
physical approval, rank calibration, sustained mobile performance or a Gold art
bar. Silver/Gold/Diamond thresholds remain provisional; Bronze has no time gate.
