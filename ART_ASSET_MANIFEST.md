## Phase 0.6.8B Rigged Hand Handedness and Material Recovery

- The same source GLB and rigged prefab paths remain active for
  `0.6.8b-hand-rig-handedness-and-material-recovery`.
- Handedness truth is explicit: the right prefab uses the source rig/mesh; the
  left prefab is generated as a baked X-mirrored mesh plus mirrored bone
  hierarchy. Runtime hierarchy scales remain positive.
- Material recovery uses role-based glTF texture import: base color is sRGB,
  normal is imported as a normal map with `_NORMALMAP`, and metallic/roughness
  is imported as linear source data but not assigned to Unity's
  `_MetallicGlossMap`.
- The rigged arm material uses metallic `0.08`, smoothness `0.42`, normal
  strength `0.35`, and backface culling. Mesh tangents are generated for both
  rigged arm meshes so the normal map lights predictably.

  left `(0, -70, 0)` and right `(0, 70, 0)`, finger curl is `18` degrees,
  thumb curl is `8` degrees, and wrist settle is reduced.
